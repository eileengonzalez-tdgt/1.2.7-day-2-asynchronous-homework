01 · Speaking · 15 min

Let's Plan an Event!

Imagine you're sending a 2–3-minute voice message to a friend or teammate about an upcoming celebration. Explain the event, where and when it is happening, whom you are inviting, and how you will handle one possible problem.

Planning prompts and useful expressions
  • What are you celebrating? Where and when are you meeting?
  • Who are you inviting? How will they contact you?
  • What are you doing before, during and after the event?
  • What if the food arrives late or the venue changes?

Useful phrases: “We're meeting on Saturday.” · “Before the party...” · “There's a problem.” · “I've got it! I'll call...”

02 · Grammar · 15 min

Future Plans or Decisions?

Use Present Progressive for arranged future events, and will for decisions made now, offers, promises and predictions. Answer all 12 questions, then check your work. You may retry.

Quick reminder

We're meeting at 6 p.m. tomorrow = an arrangement. The room is unavailable? I'll find another one = a decision made now.

03 · Writing · 10 min

Write an Invitation & Handle a Change

Part A: Write a short email or message inviting a coworker or friend to a celebration. Include the event, date, time, location and how to reply. Aim for 50–80 words.

Part B: The original venue is suddenly unavailable. Write a 30–60-word update explaining the issue, the next step and how you will confirm the new location.

Writing checklist

Compare with model messages after writing

Invitation: Hi Maya! We're having a small team celebration next Friday at 6 p.m. at the community center. We're bringing food and playing games. Would you like to join us? Please reply to this message by Wednesday so we can plan the food. I hope you can come!

Update: Hi everyone! There's a problem with the community center, so we can't use the room on Friday. I've got an idea! I'll contact another venue this afternoon. I'll email the new address as soon as it's confirmed. Thanks for your patience!

04 · Listening & Pronunciation · 15 min

Listen & Shadow: A Change of Plans

Audio provided by your trainer

Your trainer will share an audio recording with you through the designated platform. Make sure you have received it before starting. The audio is not embedded here.

Part A · Listen and understand

Listen once for the main idea. Listen again and answer the eight questions. Check your answers before revealing the transcript.

Part B · Read the transcript

Reveal the conversation transcript

Maya: Hey, Daniel! Are we still having our team celebration on Friday?

Daniel: Yes! We're meeting at the community center at six. I've already invited everyone.

Maya: That's great! Are you bringing the decorations?

Daniel: I am, but there's a problem. The community center just called. The room isn't available anymore.

Maya: Oh, no! We don't have much time to find another place.

Daniel: I know. We're running late with the preparations.

Maya: Wait, I've got it! My neighbor has a small event space. I'll call her and ask if it's available.

Daniel: That's a great idea! I'll send everyone an email once we confirm the new address.

Maya: Perfect. We're meeting tomorrow afternoon to finish planning, right?

Daniel: Exactly. And don't worry, I think everything will work out!

Maya: I hope so! See you tomorrow.

Part C · Shadowing

Replay the recording and repeat each sentence, paying attention to rhythm, connected speech and intonation. Check each phrase after practicing it aloud.
